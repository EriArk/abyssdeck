# Vision

AbyssDeck is a private project workspace connecting AI work, files, results,
GitHub, real development machines and people. The source tree and toolchain stay
on the machine where the work belongs; desktop, tablet and phone provide usable
ways to direct and inspect that work.

## The ordinary workflow

Start with an idea or a Brainstorm Room. Develop it with Project GPT, prepare the
context for Project Codex, implement against the actual checkout, inspect and edit
the resulting files, review the Git changes, then use feedback to define the next
task. Keep the exact source and project context through those handoffs.

Remote Desktop and Computer Use help when the real application needs direct
interaction. Companion supplies device connection, readiness and maintenance;
Codex is one integration, not the definition of the whole device.

## Product principles

- **Project context persists.** Conversations, checkouts, artifacts and decisions
  stay connected without becoming one unstructured chat.
- **Private work stays private.** Collaboration publishes selected material and
  uses explicit permissions; it does not share native identities or machines.
- **Useful detail stays available.** Results collects artifacts and substantive
  public progress with expandable work. Empty activity labels are live status,
  not saved reasoning. Never invent unavailable provider reasoning.
- **Recovery preserves work.** Drafts, source revisions and uncertain-operation
  receipts survive interruptions. Missing acknowledgements do not justify replay.
- **Controls fit their task and device.** Wide layouts offer adjustable panels and
  windows; narrow layouts keep navigation and touch controls usable.
- **Themes have a coherent material identity.** CRT Green and Hi-Tech 2000 feel
  like devices with attached controls; Organizer feels like a book or organizer.
- **Capabilities remain honest.** Viewing, annotation and content editing are
  different operations. A preview is not a claim of a full Office or CAD editor.

## Current scope and direction

Project Codex/GPT workflows, Files, Results, GitHub, notes/plans, collaboration,
Brainstorm and device tools have implementations. Their individual limits and
installation evidence are documented in the [feature guides](README.md) and
[CURRENT_STATUS](CURRENT_STATUS.md).

Guided self-host setup, the global cross-project AbyssDeck Assistant and completion
of independent personal Linux remain separate work. A proposal or source check
does not establish that a feature is deployed or ready for another installation.

AbyssDeck does not aim to replace every IDE, native AI client, Office/CAD tool or
communication platform. Its purpose is to preserve continuity between them while
making everyday project work practical from the device in the user's hands.
